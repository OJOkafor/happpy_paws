# 3 PROJECT OVERVIEW: 
The primary purpose of this project is to create a user-friendly and informative website for Happy Paws Animal Shelter, a no-kill, non-profit organization dedicated to rescuing and rehoming animals in need. The website will serve as a platform to promote the shelter's mission, facilitate adoptions, and engage with the community through educational resources and donation opportunities.

# 4 Goals and Objectives

The overarching goal remains to establish a robust online presence for Happy Paws Organization, fostering community support and enabling the organization to better serve animals in need.

Goal 1: Increase awareness of Happy Paws Organization and its mission.
Objective 1: Create a visually appealing website equipped with a blog, newsletter subscription, and community event gallery to increase awareness. Target 1,000 newsletter subscribers within six months and a 20% increase in event attendance.
Objective 2: Increase social media engagement by integrating social media feeds and sharing buttons on every page, aiming for a 20% increase in social media followers within three months.
Goal 2: Generate donations and increase financial support.
Objective 1: Implement a secure online donation platform with clear calls-to-action and a "Donation Impact" section. Aim to increase online donations by 25% within the next quarter.
Goal 3: Recruit volunteers and foster community involvement.
Objective 1: Develop a volunteer application form, a detailed volunteer opportunities page, and a "Team Section" showcasing staff and volunteers. Aim to increase volunteer sign-ups by 15% within two months.
Goal 4: Enhance user experience, accessibility, and engagement.
Objective 1: Simplify website navigation, ensure mobile responsiveness, and implement accessibility improvements (alt text, labels), achieving a positive user feedback score (8/10) based on user testing.

# 5  Key Features

The website will consist of five key pages, each designed with specific features and functionalities to maximize user engagement, accessibility, and achieve Happy Paws' mission. Semantic HTML5 elements, consistent class naming conventions, accessibility improvements, and responsive design are core principles.

Homepage:
Hero Section: Large welcoming banner with a background image, headline, subtext, and call-to-action button.
Featured Pets Carousel: Horizontally scrollable carousel showcasing adoptable pets with images, names, and personality descriptions.
Community Events Gallery: Grid gallery of photos from recent shelter events and volunteer activities.
Newsletter Signup Form: Simple email subscription form with input and submit button.
About Us Page:
Team Section: Photos and bios of shelter staff and volunteers using semantic containers.
Shelter Facilities Gallery: Images showing the shelter building, play areas, and medical facilities.
Before & After Rescue Section: Side-by-side images showing rescued animals' transformations.
Supporters Logos: Display logos of partner organizations and sponsors.
Adopt Page:
New Pet Entry (Leo): Added a new pet card with image, details, video, and adopt button.
Adoption Filter Bar: Form with dropdowns to filter pets by breed and age.
Adoption Success Stories: Section with photos and short stories of successfully adopted pets.
Donate Page:
Donation Impact Section: List of images with captions showing how donations help (medical care, food, shelter).
Donation Methods List: Icons and text describing ways to donate (card, PayPal, bank transfer, supplies).
Donation Form: Form to collect donor name, email, and donation amount with submit button.
Blog Page:
Blog Posts List: List of blog articles with featured images, titles (linked), excerpts, and post metadata.
Semantic Article Structure: Each post wrapped in <article> with <figure> for images and <figcaption>.
Contact Us Page:
Contact Information Section: Address, phone, email wrapped in <address>.
Contact Form: Form with fields for name, email, message, and submit button.
Embedded Google Map: Iframe showing shelter location.
Social Media Links: List of social media platforms with icons/emojis and accessible labels.
